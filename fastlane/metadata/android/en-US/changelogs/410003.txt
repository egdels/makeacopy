MakeACopy 4.10.0
* New: "OCR in the background" (Options, Scan): after cropping you go straight to Export while the text is recognised; saving waits until it is ready. Off by default.
* New: the OCR language can be chosen in the options (OCR section).
* Note: with the new option, pages imported from a multi-page PDF are not read automatically; tap their badge to run OCR.
