MakeACopy 4.7.0
* New: manual dewarping for curved book pages in the crop screen.
* New: multi-page workflow – import several PDF pages, batch OCR, memory-friendly export; unfinished documents survive a restart.
* New: re-crop any page of a multi-page document from its original photo; a curved-page crop comes back with its curves.
* Corner detection: about 9× faster; retrained model is far better with tilted documents.
* Fixed: a separate TXT file is only exported when OCR found text; the camera options no longer switch it on.
* Fixed (Paddle OCR): long justified lines of body text no longer vanish from the result; the "Best quality" option now really runs the detector at a higher resolution.
