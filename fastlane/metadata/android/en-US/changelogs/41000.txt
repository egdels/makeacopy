MakeACopy 4.10.0
* New: "OCR in the background" (Options, Scan): after cropping you go straight to Export; saving waits until the text is ready. Off by default.
* New: OCR language can be chosen in the options.
* Fixed: OCR review, Text tab: the cursor no longer jumps to the end; adding or removing words keeps the rest in place.
* Fixed: dragging a crop corner near the screen edge no longer triggers Back.
* Note: with the new option, pages from a multi-page PDF are not read automatically.
